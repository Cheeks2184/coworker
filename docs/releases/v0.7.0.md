# Coworker v0.7.0

Let your coworkers work as a team. A primary coworker coordinates the others, coworkers can hand work to each other, and Home becomes a team room that shows what everyone is doing. Web search now works right after install.

## A primary coworker

- Mark one coworker as **primary** from its settings or from the **⋯** menu in the sidebar or on the Coworkers page. It becomes your main point of contact, knows who is on the team and what they have been doing, and can hand work to the others and report back.
- The primary posts a daily **Team digest** at 9:00. Change the time on the **Schedules** page.
- Moving the role to another coworker pauses the old digest. The coworkers involved restart, and queued work is kept.

## Coworkers that work together

- Coworkers can ask each other for help. A request runs in a "From …" conversation in the other coworker's own history, and the answer comes back to the coworker who asked. Failed or cancelled requests report back too.
- Type **@** in a chat to bring in another coworker. The coworker you are chatting with summarizes the result, with the original reply shown under the summary.
- Built-in limits prevent loops: requests nest at most three deep, a pair of coworkers can send 30 requests an hour, and a coworker cannot message the coworker whose request it is answering.
- Tag coworkers, then search and filter the directory by tag. Upload a photo as a coworker's avatar.
- The primary leads the sidebar, followed by pinned coworkers, and a blinking dot shows who is working.

## The team room

- **Home** is a feed of your team's work: what is running, approvals waiting on you, and finished or delegated work. Approve, decline, or reply from a card without leaving the page.
- Filter by time (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**), status (**Needs you**, **In progress**, **Done**, **Failed**), or coworker.
- A greeting for the time of day sits above the composer. The side rail shows the current time, a **Chat with team** shortcut, approvals, and recent files.
- The feed loads more as you scroll, so long histories stay fast.

## Web search and model providers

- Web search works without a key on Firecrawl's free tier, which has a daily limit. Add a Firecrawl, Tavily, Exa, or SerpAPI key in **Settings → Web search** for higher limits.
- Settings shows when an API key is saved. **Disconnect** a model provider or **Remove key** for a search provider. Disconnecting restarts the coworkers that used the provider and clears it as the default model.
- OpenRouter keys are verified when you save them, and saving a new key restarts the coworkers that use that provider.
- The model catalog covers 375 of OpenRouter's 376 current tool-capable models, up from 296.

## Settings and navigation

- **Settings** is in the workspace menu and at the bottom of the chat sidebar.
- Settings is reorganized into **Model Providers** (OpenRouter first), **Web search**, and **Channels** (formerly Integrations).
- Removing a provider, key, endpoint, or skill asks for confirmation in an in-app dialog.
- The **Coworkers** sidebar item opens your last chat directly.
- Times use a 12-hour clock with AM/PM.
- The chat sidebar shows each coworker's role and model at a glance, and files in messages start on their own line.

## Upgrading and CLI

- Upgrading adds storage for coworker requests, tags, and photos, using the existing pre-migration backup.
- No coworker is primary until you choose one.
- `coworker models credentials remove PROVIDER` now disconnects the provider the way Settings does: it restarts the coworkers on that provider and clears it as the default model.
